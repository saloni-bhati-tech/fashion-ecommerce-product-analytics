FASHION E-COMMERCE PRODUCT ANALYTICS & CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE OPTIMIZATION

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

This project analyzes a simulated fashion e-commerce customer journey from product search and discovery to purchase and returns.

The objective is to identify customer drop-off points, understand purchasing behavior, identify potential product and checkout issues, and recommend data-driven product improvements.

Tools Used:
SQL | Excel | Power BI | Figma | Product Analytics

Dataset:
Simulated fashion e-commerce dataset containing customers, products, events, orders and returns.


2. BUSINESS PROBLEM

Fashion e-commerce platforms lose potential customers at different stages of the shopping journey.

The key business questions addressed in this project are:

• Where are customers dropping off in the purchase funnel?
• Which device has better customer conversion?
• Which product categories generate purchases?
• What payment behavior may be affecting purchase completion?
• What are the major reasons for product returns?
• What product improvements could improve customer experience and conversion?


3. CUSTOMER FUNNEL ANALYSIS

Funnel Stages:

Search → Product View → Add to Cart → Checkout → Payment → Purchase

Results:

Search: 20 users
Product View: 20 users
Add to Cart: 19 users
Checkout: 17 users
Payment: 15 users
Purchase: 8 users

Key Finding:

The largest drop-off occurs between Payment and Purchase, where approximately 47% of users do not complete the purchase after reaching the payment stage.

This indicates that checkout/payment experience should be investigated as the highest-priority product opportunity.


4. CATEGORY ANALYSIS

Product categories analyzed:

Ethnic Wear
Jeans
Dresses
Footwear
T-Shirts
Sarees
Shirts
Jackets

Key Finding:

Jeans, Dresses, Ethnic Wear and Sarees generated purchases in the simulated dataset, while T-Shirts and Jackets did not generate purchases despite receiving product interactions.

This suggests that product discovery, pricing, merchandising or recommendation strategies could be explored for low-converting categories.


5. DEVICE ANALYSIS

Devices analyzed:

Mobile
Desktop

Results:

Mobile users: 14
Mobile purchases: 6

Desktop users: 6
Desktop purchases: 2

Key Finding:

Mobile represents the majority of users and purchases in the dataset.

Because mobile is the dominant customer platform, optimizing the mobile shopping and checkout experience should be a product priority.


6. PAYMENT ANALYSIS

Payment methods analyzed:

UPI
Credit Card
COD

Results:

UPI: 7 orders, 5 completed
Credit Card: 3 orders, 2 completed
COD: 3 orders, 2 completed

Key Finding:

UPI is the most frequently used payment method in the simulated dataset.

The payment-to-purchase drop-off indicates that payment completion and checkout usability should be investigated further.


7. RETURNS ANALYSIS

Return reasons:

Size/Fit: 3
Quality Issue: 2
Color Different: 1

Key Finding:

Size/Fit is the largest return reason.

Potential product opportunity:

Improve size guides, fit recommendations and product information to help customers make more confident purchase decisions.


8. ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS

Problem 1: Payment → Purchase Drop-off

Possible causes:
• Payment failure
• Poor checkout usability
• Lack of payment feedback
• Customer hesitation before final confirmation
• Mobile checkout friction

Recommended investigation:
Analyze payment failure rates, checkout errors, loading time and abandonment behavior.


Problem 2: Size/Fit Returns

Possible causes:
• Inaccurate size expectations
• Limited size information
• Lack of fit guidance

Recommended solution:
Introduce improved size charts and personalized size/fit recommendations.


Problem 3: Low-Converting Categories

Possible causes:
• Weak product discovery
• Pricing mismatch
• Limited recommendations
• Poor visibility

Recommended solution:
Improve recommendations, category merchandising and personalized discovery.

10. USER STORIES

User Story 1:

As a shopper, I want to see relevant search suggestions so that I can discover products faster.

Acceptance Criteria:
• Search suggestions appear while typing.
• Suggestions are relevant to customer searches.
• Selecting a suggestion displays relevant products.


User Story 2:

As a shopper, I want a clear payment status so that I know whether my payment was successful.

Acceptance Criteria:
• Payment status is clearly displayed.
• Failed payments provide an understandable error message.
• Customers can retry payment easily.


User Story 3:

As a shopper, I want size recommendations so that I can select the correct size confidently.

Acceptance Criteria:
• Size guide is easily accessible.
• Recommendation uses available customer information.
• Recommended size is clearly highlighted.

Sprint 1:
Analyze funnel and identify major customer problems.

Sprint 2:
Design improved search and discovery experience.

Sprint 3:
Design checkout and payment improvements.

Sprint 4:
Evaluate KPIs and iterate based on customer behavior.


15. FINAL PRODUCT INSIGHTS

The analysis identified three major opportunities:

1. Payment-to-purchase abandonment is the largest funnel drop-off and should be investigated first.

2. Size/Fit is the most common return reason, creating an opportunity for better size and fit guidance.

3. Mobile is the dominant shopping platform, making mobile-first product optimization important.


16. PROJECT OUTCOME

This project demonstrates a product analytics workflow:

Data → SQL Analysis → Excel Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Root-Cause Analysis → Product Requirements → User Stories → Figma Prototype → KPI Definition

The project combines quantitative analysis with product thinking to identify customer problems and translate them into actionable product improvements.
